Redesigned the complete sound set shown in gameplay of Zelda BotW, Dig Dig Doom and Borderlands. The primary challenge was crafting a unique audio signature for each entity based on its element and genere. By blending heavily processed organic recordings, custom Foley, and synth layers, I built a highly varied sonic palette that gives every element, UI, magical ability, and impact a distinct and imposing presence.
