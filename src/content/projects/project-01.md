I was responsible for the complete audio pipeline—from initial musical composition and sound synthesis to the final mix—for a video mapping installation projected onto the Museu del Disseny as part of OFFF Festival 2026.

Designing audio for large-scale outdoor projections always poses serious spatial constraints and complex room dynamics. Overcoming these challenges required careful frequency carving and precise spatial positioning to ensure maximum clarity and weight across the venue. The final result was a seamlessly synchronized, powerfully striking performance that elevated the visual spectacle.
