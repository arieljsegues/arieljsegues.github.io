In collaboration with Framemov, I was responsible for the complete audio pipeline—from custom sound synthesis and foley editing to the final multi-channel mix—for an immersive installation at the Museo Minero de Teruel.

Designing audio for an underground mining exhibition requires translating the physical weight and claustrophobic acoustics of the space into a visceral sensory experience. Beyond the quadraphonic surround array (`L, R, BL, BR`) and dedicated `LFE` sub-bass channel, I engineered a discrete low-frequency tactile track routed to floor-mounted bass shakers. Overcoming the acoustic reflections of the exhibition hall required precise frequency carving and phase alignment between the airborne sub-bass and the physical floor vibrations, resulting in a deeply immersive, physically felt audiovisual experience.
