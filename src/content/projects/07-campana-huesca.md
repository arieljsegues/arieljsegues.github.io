In collaboration with Framemov, I was responsible for the complete audio pipeline—from custom sound design and Foley editing to the final 5.1 surround mix—for La campana de Huesca, a four-wall immersive projection installation.

Designing audio for an enclosed 360-degree projection room requires translating a shifting dramatic narrative—from intimate details like footsteps and pen strokes to high-tension climaxes—into a cohesive spatial environment. Utilizing a standard 5.1 surround array (`L, R, C, LFE, BL, BR`), I focused on precise frequency carving, dynamic balance, and spatial panning across all four walls so that sound sources moved seamlessly around the audience, enveloping the room to heighten the narrative tension.
