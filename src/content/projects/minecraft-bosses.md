Designed the complete sound set for a Minecraft add-on centered on custom boss encounters. The primary challenge was crafting a unique audio signature for each entity based on its element, physical scale, and level of hostility—ranging from massive, slow-rumbling earth colossi to fast, volatile elemental creatures. By blending heavily processed organic recordings, custom Foley, and synth layers, I built a highly varied sonic palette that gives every roar, magical ability, and impact a distinct and imposing presence.
