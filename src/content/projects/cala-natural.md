I crafted and mixed the sound design for the L'Ametlla video mapping, conceived as a high-octane, large-scale visual spectacle. The primary hurdle was cutting through complex outdoor ambient noise while retaining microscopic audio detail. Through surgical frequency carving and painstaking sound design, I delivered a crisp, high-impact mix with massive presence that fully commanded the outdoor space.
