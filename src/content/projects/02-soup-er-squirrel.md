For Soup-er Squirrel (https://yaygun.itch.io/soup-er-squirrel), a relaxing cooking game set in autumn, I was responsible for the sound design and audio implementation in FMOD.

The main challenge was creating an audio experience that complemented the game’s cozy atmosphere while providing satisfying and intuitive feedback for the player’s actions. I focused on designing sounds that felt natural, warm, and cohesive, enhancing the cooking interactions and reinforcing the relaxing pace of the gameplay.

I also handled the implementation of all audio elements in FMOD, ensuring they integrated smoothly with the game and contributed to an immersive and cohesive soundscape.

The final result was a warm, cozy audio experience that supports the game’s autumnal setting and makes every cooking interaction feel satisfying.
