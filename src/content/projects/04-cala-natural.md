In collaboration with Framemov, I was responsible for the complete audio pipeline—from custom sound design and editing to the final stereo mix and mastering—for La Cala més natural, a large-scale architectural video mapping in L'Ametlla de Mar.

Designing and mixing audio for an open-air public square poses significant acoustic challenges, particularly competing against unpredictable crowd noise and complex outdoor reflections. To ensure maximum intelligibility and impact in a hostile acoustic environment, I engineered a punchy, tightly controlled stereo mix and master—focusing on surgical frequency carving, midrange clarity, and dynamic control so that every sonic detail cut cleanly through the ambient noise without losing weight across the plaza.
